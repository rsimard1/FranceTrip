GR52 Hike Day
🥾
GR52: Castellar → Plan du Lion — challenging morning climb, 2.5–3 hrs, panoramic views spanning France, Monaco, and Italy. Well-marked trail. Early start recommended to beat the heat.
🌿
Afternoon option A: Jardin Botanique Val Rahmeh — lush tropical botanical garden a short walk from the beach. Free on the first Sunday of the month. Free entry

Les Enfants Terribles  Booked · 7:30pm
On Quai Bonaparte by the Jean Cocteau Museum. Mediterranean fish and seafood, colorful presentation, sea views from the terrace. 1 Quai Bonaparte, Menton.
Monday, June 15
Èze & Monaco
🏔️
Morning — Èze (30 min drive): Medieval village perched at 429m. Take the Nietzsche Path up from Èze-sur-Mer for the best approach — steep but spectacular. Allow 2–3 hours to explore. Very touristy but worth it.
🍽️
Lunch in Èze — Château Eza or Nid d'Aigle both have dramatic cliffside terraces.
🎰
Afternoon/Evening — Monaco (20 min from Èze): Casino square, the harbour yachts, Prince's Palace, F1 circuit streets. A few hours is plenty. Drinks at a harbour bar before heading back.
Dinner — confirmed reservation (Monaco)
🍽️
Maya Bay  Booked · 7:00pm
Michelin-listed Thai-influenced restaurant in the heart of Monaco. Excellent atmosphere, great terrace, and a fun change of pace after a full day exploring the Principality. Stay in Monaco for the evening — no need to drive back to Menton for dinner.
Tuesday, June 16
Cap Ferrat & coastal walk
🚶
Cap Ferrat — walk the 10km coastal path ringing the peninsula. Spectacular Belle Époque villas, rocky coves, turquoise water. One of the great free walks on the Riviera.
🍽️
Lunch at Four Seasons Grand-Hôtel du Cap-Ferrat — poolside terrace. Outstanding food, legendary people-watching, and not as punishing at lunch as you'd expect. Make a reservation. Reserve lunch
🚂
Tip: the local train from Menton runs cheaply and frequently to Monaco, Cap Ferrat, and Nice. Consider leaving the car and going stress-free.
Dinner — confirmed reservation
🍽️
Le Bistrot des Jardins  Booked · 7:30pm
Villa garden terrace on Jardin Biovès. Michelin guide-listed for over a decade. Provençal and Mediterranean classics — braised lamb, grilled John Dory, excellent wine list. 14 Avenue Boyer, Menton.
June 17
Transit Day — Saint-Tropez
Menton to Hyères via the coast · ~3 hrs driving · Saint-Tropez stop en route
Wednesday, June 17
Drive West & Saint-Tropez
🚗
Drive from Menton to Hyères via A8 or the Corniche — approximately 2.5–3 hours without stops. Leave early to make the most of Saint-Tropez.
⛵
Saint-Tropez — all four of you: Park on the edge of town and walk in. The port is the heart of it — yachts, cafés, people-watching. Allow a full afternoon.
🚶
Explore La Ponche, the old fishermen's quarter — narrow cobblestone lanes, quieter than the port, great for wandering. Citadelle de Saint-Tropez has sweeping views over the bay.
☕
Place des Lices for a coffee or pastis under the plane trees — the classic Saint-Tropez square, pétanque optional.
🏨
Continue to Hyères (~1 hr) and check in — confirm accommodation near Carqueiranne village for easy access to the château.

Friday, June 19
Free Day — Pick Your Adventure
Allie may have rehearsal dinner — confirm with her. Options for the group:
🏝️
Option A — Île de Porquerolles: Car-free island, white sand beaches, great snorkelling, and national park trails. ~30 min ferry from La Tour Fondue on the Giens peninsula. The most scenic day-trip from Hyères — best done as a full day. Check ferry times
🥾
Option B — Gorge du Verdon: The "Grand Canyon of Europe" — brilliant turquoise water, dramatic limestone cliffs. ~1.5 hrs from Hyères. Kayak or canoe on the lake, or hike the Sentier Martel along the gorge rim. A full-day commitment but unforgettable.
🏖️
Option C — Beach & Bormes-les-Mimosas: Morning at Plage de l'Almanarre or Plage du Pellegrin (wilder, more remote), then drive 30 min to Bormes-les-Mimosas — a medieval hilltop village draped in bougainvillea. Lunch in the village square.
🏙️
Option D — Hyères Vieille Ville + wine: Leisurely morning exploring Place Massillon and the medieval streets, then an afternoon wine tasting at Château du Galoupet or Château Sainte Marguerite — both in the hills above Hyères and well-regarded Provence rosé producers.
Dinner — last dinner before wedding

La Petite Galinette
Top-rated Provençal bistro — the locals' favourite. Fresh seasonal produce, refined cuisine, terrace seating. Reserve ahead.
🍽️
